Welcome to your financial literacy journey! This book is designed to sharpen your
knowledge while you have fun. Whether you’re learning about Earning, Budgeting,
Saving, or Investing, these puzzles will help you master the lingo. 
Instructions 
Crossword Puzzles 
1. Fill the white squares with the correct financial terms. 
2. How to Play: Read the "Across" and "Down" clues. Each box takes only one 
letter. 
3. Pro Tip: Start with the easy ones first to get "anchor" letters for the harder 
words. 
Word Banks 
Use these as your "cheat sheet" for word searches or when you're stuck on a 
crossword clue. They contain all the terms you'll need for that specific page. 
Word Search Boxes 
1. Find all the hidden financial words from the word bank. 
2. Words can be hidden forward, backward, up, down, or even diagonally. 
3. Pro Tip: Cross off the words in your word bank as you find them so you don't 
get confused. 
Cryptograms 
1. Decode a hidden words or financial facts in these puzzles. 
2. Using the A1Z26 cipher, replace each number with the corresponding letter in 
the provided table. 
Before you start, grab a pencil and an eraser instead of a pen. A pencil lets you 
erase and try again without leaving a messy page. 
Stuck? 
If a puzzle really has you stuck for ideas, flip to the Answer Key at the very back 
of the book. Use it to learn the term, and then try the next puzzle on your own!

Kenya to prevent counterfeiting. For example, you could close your eyes and feel 
the tactile bars on the edges of the notes. The number of bars corresponds to 
the note’s value e.g., 1 bar for Ksh. 50 and 5 bars for Ksh. 1,000.

Fun Activity 
Before common languages like Kiswahili developed, merchants practiced "Silent
Trade" to avoid conflict.
The Task: Place an "export" item e.g., a book or a snack on a table. Your parent 
i.e. the foreign merchant, will place a "payment" item next to it. 
The Rule: Without speaking, you must either take the payment i.e. accepting 
the deal, or walk away and wait for them to add more to the pile until you are 
satisfied.
The Lesson: This reflects how gold and salt were traded across the Sahara 
and the Indian Ocean for centuries without a shared language
